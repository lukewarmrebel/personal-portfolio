Case study 02InsuranceUS + India

The claim is the product

A GenAI claims assistant for personal lines. Report on WhatsApp or by text, get paid in minutes when the claim is simple, and get a real person when it isn’t.

Download the full PDF9-page PDF · October 2026
How I would build it. Not something I shipped.
Where I draw on my own work, it’s from my time as a PM in motor insurance at ICICI Lombard.
01

The problem

Ravi is 29. He scraped his hatchback against a pillar in his office basement in Hyderabad. He thinks in Telugu, types in English and lives on WhatsApp.

He has never heard the words “intimation” or “surveyor”. He wants three answers. Am I covered? How much will I get? Can I go to my own garage?

Megan, in Ohio, got rear-ended at a light with two kids in the back. She wants to file from the parking lot and not tell the story to three different people.

People buy insurance for one moment, the claim. And that’s exactly where we hand them the most forms. I’ve watched this from the inside, so this one is personal.

02

US vs India

Different claims, different clocks

India puts hard clocks on a motor claim

IRDAI master circular, 2024. The surveyor is late? The claimant gets ₹500 a day.

24 h
surveyor allocated15 days
survey report due7 days
insurer decides

Cashless health is faster still: 1 hour to decide, 3 hours for final approval at discharge. And no claim can be rejected for want of documents.

That last rule shapes the product. The assistant has to collect everything in one go, and it can never close a claim because the customer went quiet. Chasing documents is our job, not theirs.

05

Decisions I’d defend in a review

WhatsApp first, not the insurer’s app

Bajaj General’s Motor OTS already settles car claims up to ₹30,000 in 20 minutes, in its app. That’s my benchmark. It also shows me the gap: doing it in WhatsApp, in Telugu, for the customer who will never download an insurer’s app.

Push everyone to download the app firstLoses the people who need it most.

Rules decide coverage, models just read

Policy terms are rules. They live in a deterministic engine that I can audit line by line, same pattern as the admissibility engine I owned. The LLM understands what happened. It doesn’t decide what’s covered.

Let the LLM read the wording and decideCan’t audit it, can’t explain it to a regulator.

Start with own damage under ₹50,000

No surveyor needed by rule, high volume, visible damage. I learn fastest here. Lakshmi’s 40 pages of hospital bills matter too, but health reimbursement comes in phase 2.

Launch with health and motor togetherTwo eval sets, two ops teams, half the learning.

Three months of shadow before any payout

Reporting and extraction go live. The AI recommends and people decide. Automated payouts start under low caps, and caps only rise on audit data.

Turn on payouts in month oneLeakage would show up before I could measure it.

From my work on DIY inspection: adoption went up 30% after we fixed the friction points in the self-serve flow. In self-serve, the experience around the model matters as much as the model.
06

How I’d know it works

Metrics tree

It joins speed and correctness in one number. You can’t hit it by paying everything fast, because leakage and reopens would show up. You can’t hit it by sending every claim to a person, because speed would drop.

North star

Simple claims settled right, fast

Share of eligible claims paid within 24 hours of first report, and not reopened or complained about within 60 days.

Customers report digitally

  • Digital first-report share
  • Report completion rate, by language

Files are complete

  • Docs complete in one go
  • STP rate on eligible claims

People step in fast

  • Time to first human touch
  • Handoff SLA met

Guardrails. Any one of these can stop a rollout.

  • Payout leakage vs 100% audit
  • Fraud slip-through
  • Complaints and reopens
  • Fairness: no language group 5+ pts behind
  • Handoff SLA
07

Roadmap

Each phase has a gate
Months 0 to 3

Shadow

Reporting and extraction live. The AI recommends, people decide. Nothing paid automatically.

Gate: extraction bars met, 90%+ shadow agreement
Months 3 to 6

STP lite

Automated payouts under low caps. Fraud lanes. Status updates without the customer asking.

Gate: leakage within audit tolerance, complaints not rising
Months 6 to 12

Expand

India: health reimbursement intake, cashless status. US: home water and wind. Voice in the call centre. Caps rise toward ₹50,000.

Gate: each new line passes its own evals
Month 12+

Rails

Bima Sugam and NHCX hooks, garage and hospital-side flows, catastrophe surge mode.

Gate: ongoing
08

First 90 days

Days 1 to 30 · baseline

Today’s funnel by claim type. Label about 2,000 past claims per market. Listen to 50 first-report calls before writing a single prompt.

Days 31 to 60 · shadow

Report completion by language, extraction accuracy, shadow agreement with adjusters, and how many fraud flags the fraud team agrees with.

Days 61 to 90 · go or no-go

If the bars are met, payouts under the lowest cap in one city and one state. One page a week to claims leadership.

What would make me stop: leakage above audit tolerance two weeks in a row, or any language group falling more than 5 points behind on completion.
The model is the easy part to buy. The product is the judgement about where it’s allowed to act.
